# KAVACH AI — Platform Limitations & Legal Disclaimer

## 1. Regulatory & Technical Position
KAVACH AI is strictly an **AI-assisted decision-support system** designed to aid security operations centre (SOC) analysts, enterprise fraud investigators, and authorized operators.

> **CRITICAL LEGAL NOTICE**: KAVACH AI is **NOT** a guaranteed fraud detector and does not make legally binding judicial determinations. Real-world fraud allegations must always be independently corroborated by authorized law enforcement agencies.

---

## 2. Ingestion & Interception Boundaries
1. **No Direct Telecommunications Interception**:
   - KAVACH AI does **NOT** tap, intercept, or snoop on cellular telephony networks (GSM, LTE, 5G) or telecommunications carriers.
   - It operates strictly upon audio files uploaded with user consent or microphone audio captured with operating system permissions.
2. **Audio Quality Sensitivity**:
   - Extreme background acoustic distortion, heavy reverberation, or overlapping cross-talk can degrade ASR word error rate (WER) and diminish keyword matching precision.
3. **Dialectal & Slang Variation**:
   - While code-mixed Telugu, Hindi, and English are supported, highly localized regional idioms or coded slang not present in the pattern dictionary may require manual rule updates.
4. **Synthetic Benchmark Caveat**:
   - Performance benchmarks conducted on the 20-scenario synthetic dataset demonstrate algorithm logic and rule coverage, but do not statistically guarantee equivalent real-world field accuracy across all demographic contexts.

---

## 3. Emergency Incident Protocol
In situations involving immediate financial fraud, active coercion, or physical danger:
- **Do not rely solely on automated software warnings.**
- Immediately disconnect the phone or video call.
- Dial the **National Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930** (Government of India).
- File an official complaint on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: **https://cybercrime.gov.in**.
- Report stolen cards or unauthorized transactions directly to your bank's 24/7 toll-free helpline.
