## 1. Model Summary

The **KAVACH-AI TF-IDF Multilingual Calibrated Classifier v3.0** is a lightweight, low-latency machine learning model engineered for real-time detection of social engineering threats, vishing attacks, and financial scams in telephony conversations. 

Trained on the 2,100-sample training partition of **Dataset v3**, the model maps conversational transcripts to a 3-class probability distribution:
- `LEGITIMATE` (Class 0): Routine, non-malicious customer service, banking inquiries, or security awareness.
- `AMBIGUOUS` (Class 1): Gray-area conversations, suspicious urgency without explicit demands, or edge-case verifications requiring further telemetry.
- `SCAM` (Class 2): High-confidence coercion, digital arrest threats, illegal money transfer requests, or OTP harvesting.

The model incorporates **Platt scaling** (sigmoid calibration via 3-fold cross validation) to transform raw decision margins into well-calibrated posterior probabilities, enabling principled uncertainty estimation and downstream risk fusion.

---

## 2. Intended Use

### Primary Intended Uses
- **Real-Time Stream Classification**: Tier-1 edge evaluation of streaming telephony transcripts generated by speech-to-text (Gnani Prisma STT).
- **Hybrid Risk Fusion**: Serving as the probabilistic component of KAVACH AI's hybrid threat engine, fused with deterministic heuristic rules under the **Rule-Priority Shield**.
- **Edge / Resource-Constrained Telephony Gateways**: Running on commodity CPU telephony servers with sub-5 ms latency budgets and minimal RAM footprint.

### Out-of-Scope & Prohibited Uses
- **Autonomous Call Disconnection Without Safeguards**: The model must **never** be used to unilaterally disconnect emergency calls or high-priority communications without the deterministic Rule-Priority Shield.
- **Sole Source of Truth for Legal Adjudication**: Predictions cannot serve as certified evidence of criminal culpability without human SOC investigator review.
- **Audio-Only Inference**: Model operates on text transcripts; it does not analyze acoustic pitch, stress, or speaker biometrics.

## 4. Training Data & Methodology

### Dataset Provenance
- **Source**: `data/kavach_synthetic_v3/splits/`
- **Total Dataset Size**: 3,000 synthetic records
  - **Train**: 2,100 records (70%) — 840 SCAM, 630 LEGITIMATE, 630 AMBIGUOUS
  - **Validation**: 450 records (15%) — 180 SCAM, 135 LEGITIMATE, 135 AMBIGUOUS
  - **Test**: 450 records (15%) — 180 SCAM, 135 LEGITIMATE, 135 AMBIGUOUS
- **Scenario Families**: 600 unique scenario families (0% cross-split leakage).
- **Annotation Status**: 100% of records marked `annotation_status: pending_review`. No human annotations.

### Training Procedure
1. **Feature Extraction**: Word n-grams `(1, 2)` with sublinear term-frequency scaling (`sublinear_tf=True`), unicode accent stripping, and `max_features=5,000`. Fitted strictly on the training partition.
2. **Base Optimization**: Logistic Regression with `C=1.5`, balanced class weighting (`class_weight="balanced"`), L2 penalty, and L-BFGS solver.
3. **Probability Calibration**: `CalibratedClassifierCV(method="sigmoid", cv=3)` fitted on the training split to produce Platt scaling parameters.

## 7. Ethical Considerations, Safeguards & Limitations

1. **Synthetic Data Disclosure**: All training and test dialogues are synthetic domain representations. Metrics reflect performance against these structured templates. Real-world wild telephony calls may suffer from ambient noise, lossy speech compression (AMR-NB 8kHz), regional dialect shifts, and speech recognition transcription errors.
2. **Annotation Verification Boundary**: Records carry `annotation_status: pending_review`. Human cybersecurity SOC review must be incorporated prior to active live enforcement.
3. **Rule-Priority Shield Mandate**: The model must never operate in isolation. In the KAVACH AI architecture, deterministic rules for critical threats (such as Digital Arrest and CBI extortion) take absolute precedence over ML predictions, ensuring high-consequence attacks cannot be bypassed by ML false negatives.
4. **Data Privacy**: No real subscriber or personal identifiable information (PII) was used in the training of this model.
