=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH-008 (en) ===
Scenario: Telecom Authority SIM Card Block Threat & Verification Charge
Primary Label: SCAM
Tactics: AUTHORITY_IMPERSONATION, URGENCY_OR_THREAT, FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION

[CALLER]: Automated notification from Department of Telecommunications. All services on your primary mobile number will be terminated within two hours.
[RECIPIENT]: Terminated? What is the reason?
[CALLER]: Thirteen complaints of harassment and illegal financial SMS broadcasting have been lodged against this SIM card in New Delhi.
[RECIPIENT]: I live in Chennai! I have never sent any illegal SMS.
[CALLER]: Your Aadhaar has been duplicated. To prevent a permanent national telecom blacklist, pay a security bond of nine thousand rupees via UPI.
[RECIPIENT]: Why should I pay a security bond to stop telecom disconnection?
[CALLER]: This is refundable bail money sir. If you refuse, telecom enforcement and Delhi police will arrest you tomorrow.
[RECIPIENT]: DoT never collects bail money via UPI. I am lodging an official fraud complaint on Chakshu portal.
