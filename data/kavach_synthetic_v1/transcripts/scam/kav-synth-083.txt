=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH-083 (hi-en) ===
Scenario: Fake Electricity Bill Penalty APK Download Threat
Primary Label: SCAM
Tactics: REMOTE_ACCESS_MANIPULATION, MALICIOUS_LINK_OR_APP, URGENCY_OR_THREAT, AUTHORITY_IMPERSONATION

[CALLER]: Third party payment app ka server error tha sir, unka reconciliation hamare electricity board mein pending hai.
[RECIPIENT]: Toh main subah bill receipt electricity office dikha dunga.
[CALLER]: Night line maintenance squad already on the way hai. Line chalu rakhne ke liye hamara quick utility APK update install kijiye.
[RECIPIENT]: APK file kahan se milegi?
[CALLER]: Hum WhatsApp par direct link bhej rahe hain. Install karke bank details fill karke one rupee verification payment kijiye.
[RECIPIENT]: Third-party APK se phone control lene ka scam hai yeh. Main direct discom substation call karunga.
