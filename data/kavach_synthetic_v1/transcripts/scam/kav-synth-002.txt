=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH-002 (en) ===
Scenario: SBI Netbanking Credential Harvesting & KYC Lock Threat
Primary Label: SCAM
Tactics: ACCOUNT_OR_KYC_THREAT, OTP_PIN_CVV_THEFT, MALICIOUS_LINK_OR_APP, URGENCY_OR_THREAT

[CALLER]: Good morning sir, I am calling from State Bank of India regulatory compliance cell.
[RECIPIENT]: Yes, what is the matter?
[CALLER]: Your savings account KYC documents have expired as per RBI guidelines. Your debit card and UPI will be blocked in two hours.
[RECIPIENT]: I visited my home branch last month and submitted my PAN card.
[CALLER]: Our central database shows pending digital verification. Please open the SMS link sent to your phone and enter your netbanking username and password.
[RECIPIENT]: Why should I enter my login password on a link sent via SMS?
[CALLER]: It is an encrypted portal sir. If you delay, your funds will be frozen and you will face a penalty of five thousand rupees.
[RECIPIENT]: No legitimate bank asks for passwords over phone or SMS. I will report this number.
