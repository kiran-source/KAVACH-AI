=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH-085 (hi-en) ===
Scenario: Customs Duty Fee Demand for International Gift Parcel
Primary Label: SCAM
Tactics: COURIER_CUSTOMS_FRAUD, AUTHORITY_IMPERSONATION, URGENCY_OR_THREAT, FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION

[CALLER]: Hello, this is Custom Clearance Officer Neha calling from Indira Gandhi International Airport cargo facility.
[RECIPIENT]: Yes, kaun bol rahe hain?
[CALLER]: Aapke name par London se ek diplomatic gift parcel arrive hua hai containing gold watch, iPhone 16 and twenty thousand UK pounds.
[RECIPIENT]: Mujhe London se koi gift nahi bhejta. Yeh kisi aur ka hoga.
[CALLER]: Box par aapka mobile number aur address clearly printed hai. Foreign currency illegal undeclared smuggling hai under FEMA Act.
[RECIPIENT]: Maine koi parcel order nahi kiya!
[CALLER]: FIR avoid karne ke liye custom clearance tax of thirty-eight thousand rupees transfer kijiye hamare RBI officer ke Google Pay par.
[RECIPIENT]: Customs tax kabhi Google Pay par personal account mein nahi liya jata. Fraud call hai yeh.
