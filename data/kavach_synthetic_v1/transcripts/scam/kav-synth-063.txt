=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH-063 (te-en) ===
Scenario: Fake Credit Card Limit Enhancement OTP Theft
Primary Label: SCAM
Tactics: OTP_PIN_CVV_THEFT, ACCOUNT_OR_KYC_THREAT, AUTHORITY_IMPERSONATION

[CALLER]: Good morning sir, calling from HDFC Bank credit card operations department.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello, yes tell me.
[CALLER]: Sir, mee Regalia credit card limit two lakhs nundi five lakhs ki upgrade cheyadaniki approval vachindi.
[RECIPIENT]: Any additional annual charges for limit increase?
[CALLER]: Zero annual charges sir, it is a lifetime free upgrade. Just card verification process complete cheyali.
[RECIPIENT]: Okay, process enti?
[CALLER]: Mee registered mobile number ki six digit OTP send chesamu, limit upgrade kosam aa code cheppandi.
[RECIPIENT]: Message lo transaction OTP ani undi! Bank credit limit ki OTP adagadu. You are trying to steal my money.
