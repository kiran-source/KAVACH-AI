=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH-005 (en) ===
Scenario: Work-From-Home YouTube Rating Advance Fee Scam
Primary Label: SCAM
Tactics: FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION, URGENCY_OR_THREAT

[CALLER]: Hi, I am HR executive Natasha from Global Digital Media Solutions. We saw your resume on Shine.com.
[RECIPIENT]: Okay, which role is this regarding?
[CALLER]: Part-time digital rating evaluator. You only need to like and subscribe to travel videos. Daily payout is three thousand rupees.
[RECIPIENT]: Sounds very easy. How does payment work?
[CALLER]: We already sent three hundred rupees trial bonus. To activate the VIP payroll gateway, you need to pay a refundable security deposit of two thousand rupees.
[RECIPIENT]: Why should an employee pay money to start work?
[CALLER]: It is standard company registration compliance. Once you transfer, your account will be activated instantly with your first salary.
[RECIPIENT]: Legitimate employers never ask for registration fees. This is a task scam.
