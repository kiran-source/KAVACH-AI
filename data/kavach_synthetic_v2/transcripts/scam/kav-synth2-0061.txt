=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0061 (en) ===
Scenario: EPFO Pension Release & NetBanking Credential Phishing (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: AUTHORITY_IMPERSONATION, OTP_PIN_CVV_THEFT, ACCOUNT_OR_KYC_THREAT

[CALLER]: This is Officer Srivastava calling from EPFO Regional Cell. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding pension arrears of 1.4 lakhs pending release.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any pension arrears of 1.4 lakhs pending release. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be lapse of provident fund interest benefits.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with net banking user ID and OTP verification. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
