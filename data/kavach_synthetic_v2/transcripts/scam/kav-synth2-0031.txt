=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0031 (en) ===
Scenario: Electricity Arrears & Power Disconnection Emergency (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: URGENCY_OR_THREAT, FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION, ACCOUNT_OR_KYC_THREAT

[CALLER]: This is Officer Shinde calling from State Electricity Board. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding 15 rupees outstanding surcharge from prior cycle.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any 15 rupees outstanding surcharge from prior cycle. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be power supply termination in 30 minutes.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with net banking password via urgent link. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
