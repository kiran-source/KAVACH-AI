=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0146 (en) ===
Scenario: Cyber Police Obscene Content Complaint Extortion (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: AUTHORITY_IMPERSONATION, URGENCY_OR_THREAT, FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION

[CALLER]: This is Officer Sengupta calling from Cyber Crime Women Cell. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding obscene video call complaint registered against your phone.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any obscene video call complaint registered against your phone. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be filing of non-bailable public defamation charge.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with case settlement fee to police welfare account. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
