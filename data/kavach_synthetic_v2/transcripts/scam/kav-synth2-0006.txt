=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0006 (en) ===
Scenario: Customs Narcotics International Parcel Confiscation (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: COURIER_CUSTOMS_FRAUD, AUTHORITY_IMPERSONATION, URGENCY_OR_THREAT, FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION

[CALLER]: This is Officer Deshmukh calling from Mumbai Airport Customs. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding FedEx carton seized with MDMA drugs.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any FedEx carton seized with MDMA drugs. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be Anti-Narcotics Bureau immediate remand.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with provisional customs clearance bond. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
