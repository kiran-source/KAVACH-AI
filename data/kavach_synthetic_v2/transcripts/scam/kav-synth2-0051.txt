=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0051 (en) ===
Scenario: Supreme Court Contempt Notice & Asset Freezing Coercion (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: AUTHORITY_IMPERSONATION, URGENCY_OR_THREAT, FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION

[CALLER]: This is Advocate Tandon calling from Supreme Court Registry. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding contempt of court summons regarding unpaid digital fine.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any contempt of court summons regarding unpaid digital fine. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be seizure of immovable family assets.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with court treasury security deposit. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
