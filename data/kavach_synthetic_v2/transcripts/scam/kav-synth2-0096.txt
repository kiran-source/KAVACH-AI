=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0096 (en) ===
Scenario: Instant Personal Loan Approval Processing Fee Extortion (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION, URGENCY_OR_THREAT

[CALLER]: This is Officer Agarwal calling from QuickFin Lending Desk. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding pre-approved unsecured loan of 5 lakhs sanctioned.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any pre-approved unsecured loan of 5 lakhs sanctioned. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be forfeiture of zero percent interest loan approval.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with 9,800 rupees insurance stamp duty fee. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
