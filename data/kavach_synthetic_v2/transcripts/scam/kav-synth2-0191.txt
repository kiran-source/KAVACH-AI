=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0191 (en) ===
Scenario: Fake Anti-Virus Subscription Renewal & Remote Refund Scam (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: REMOTE_ACCESS_MANIPULATION, FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION, URGENCY_OR_THREAT

[CALLER]: This is Tech Lead Peter calling from Global Cyber Defender. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding automatic annual renewal fee of 24,000 charged to account.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any automatic annual renewal fee of 24,000 charged to account. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be non-refundable auto-debit if not cancelled in 1 hour.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with TeamViewer remote connection to process refund. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
