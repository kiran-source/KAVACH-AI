=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0151 (en) ===
Scenario: Cryptocurrency Investment Guaranteed Double Return Trap (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION, URGENCY_OR_THREAT

[CALLER]: This is Trader Aryan calling from BitPro Wealth Advisory. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding algorithmic Bitcoin trading giving 200% return in 24 hours.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any algorithmic Bitcoin trading giving 200% return in 24 hours. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be expiration of guaranteed profit multiplier window.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with initial investment deposit of 20,000 rupees. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
