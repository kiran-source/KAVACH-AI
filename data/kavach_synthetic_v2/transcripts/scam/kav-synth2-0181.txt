=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0181 (en) ===
Scenario: Gas Cylinder Booking Express Verification Fee Fraud (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION, OTP_PIN_CVV_THEFT, URGENCY_OR_THREAT

[CALLER]: This is Officer Yadav calling from HP Gas Distributor. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding cylinder delivery delayed due to pending safety verification.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any cylinder delivery delayed due to pending safety verification. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be permanent suspension of residential gas connection.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with advance safety token payment via unknown link. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
