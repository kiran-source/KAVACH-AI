=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0121 (en) ===
Scenario: Gold Loan Default Imminent Auction Settlement Coercion (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: URGENCY_OR_THREAT, FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION, ACCOUNT_OR_KYC_THREAT

[CALLER]: This is Manager Nair calling from Muthoot Asset Desk. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding pending interest default on pawned gold ornaments.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any pending interest default on pawned gold ornaments. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be public auction of family gold jewelry tomorrow.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with immediate UPI transfer of overdue interest. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
