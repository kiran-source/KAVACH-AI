=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0091 (en) ===
Scenario: Gas Pipeline Subsidy Release & Mobile QuickSupport Trap (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: REMOTE_ACCESS_MANIPULATION, ACCOUNT_OR_KYC_THREAT, OTP_PIN_CVV_THEFT

[CALLER]: This is Officer Kaul calling from Indane Gas Vigilance. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding accumulated subsidy of 4,800 rupees held in pipeline.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any accumulated subsidy of 4,800 rupees held in pipeline. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be cancellation of domestic LPG cylinder quota.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with TeamViewer QuickSupport screen sharing. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
