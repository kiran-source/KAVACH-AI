=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0186 (en) ===
Scenario: School Tuition Fee Payment Gateway Interception Trap (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION, URGENCY_OR_THREAT

[CALLER]: This is Cashier Mohan calling from Delhi Public School Accounts. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding pending school tuition installment for current semester.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any pending school tuition installment for current semester. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be removal of student name from examination roster.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with direct transfer to private accountant UPI handle. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
