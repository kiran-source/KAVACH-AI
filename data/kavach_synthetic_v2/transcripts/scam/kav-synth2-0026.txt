=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0026 (en) ===
Scenario: Debit Card Expiry & Urgent OTP Harvest Phishing (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: ACCOUNT_OR_KYC_THREAT, OTP_PIN_CVV_THEFT, URGENCY_OR_THREAT

[CALLER]: This is Manager Rajesh calling from SBI Card Operations. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding global chip debit card expiration tonight at midnight.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any global chip debit card expiration tonight at midnight. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be permanent suspension of Google Pay and ATM.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with 6-digit card renewal authorization OTP. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
