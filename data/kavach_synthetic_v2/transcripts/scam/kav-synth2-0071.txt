=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0071 (en) ===
Scenario: Credit Card Reward Points Expiry Phishing APK (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: MALICIOUS_LINK_OR_APP, OTP_PIN_CVV_THEFT, ACCOUNT_OR_KYC_THREAT

[CALLER]: This is Officer Mehra calling from ICICI Bank Rewards Cell. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding accumulated reward points worth 14,200 rupees expiring.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any accumulated reward points worth 14,200 rupees expiring. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be forfeiture of loyalty reward cash balance.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with custom APK installation to claim cash. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
