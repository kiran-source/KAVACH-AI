Scenario: Defence Personnel Vehicle Advance Token Payment Fraud (EN)

[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with 15,000 rupees military gate pass token. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
