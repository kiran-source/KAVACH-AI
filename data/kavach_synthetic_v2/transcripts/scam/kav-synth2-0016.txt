=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0016 (en) ===
Scenario: State Cyber Crime Cell Section 420 Contempt Warrant (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: AUTHORITY_IMPERSONATION, URGENCY_OR_THREAT, ISOLATION_OR_PSYCHOLOGICAL_PRESSURE

[CALLER]: This is Inspector Kulkarni calling from Cyber Crime Branch. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding phishing portal operated using your registered IP.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any phishing portal operated using your registered IP. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be police patrol team dispatched to residence.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with confidential video link interrogation. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
