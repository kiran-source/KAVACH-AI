=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0011 (en) ===
Scenario: TRAI Mobile SIM Disconnection & Fake Aadhaar Abuse (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: AUTHORITY_IMPERSONATION, ACCOUNT_OR_KYC_THREAT, URGENCY_OR_THREAT, OTP_PIN_CVV_THEFT

[CALLER]: This is Officer Mathur calling from Telecom Regulatory Authority. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding 9 unauthorized SIM cards sending abusive fraud messages.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any 9 unauthorized SIM cards sending abusive fraud messages. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be nationwide mobile connection block within 2 hours.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with Aadhaar verification OTP code. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
