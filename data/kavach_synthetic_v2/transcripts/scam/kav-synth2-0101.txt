=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0101 (en) ===
Scenario: Mobile SIM 5G Upgrade & Remote Screen Hijacking (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: REMOTE_ACCESS_MANIPULATION, ACCOUNT_OR_KYC_THREAT, URGENCY_OR_THREAT

[CALLER]: This is Executive Roy calling from Airtel Technical Desk. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding mandatory migration of 4G SIM to 5G network.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any mandatory migration of 4G SIM to 5G network. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be permanent SIM network deactivation tonight.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with SMS forwarding code and screen sharing approval. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
