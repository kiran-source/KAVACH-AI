=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0176 (en) ===
Scenario: Insurance Bonus Settlement AnyDesk Verification Scam (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: REMOTE_ACCESS_MANIPULATION, OTP_PIN_CVV_THEFT, FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION

[CALLER]: This is Officer Tripathi calling from LIC Claims Division. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding accrued life insurance loyalty bonus of 2.1 lakhs.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any accrued life insurance loyalty bonus of 2.1 lakhs. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be forfeiture of accumulated policy maturity bonus.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with AnyDesk code sharing to verify bank passbook. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
