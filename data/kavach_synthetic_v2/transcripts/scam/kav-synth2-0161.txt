=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0161 (en) ===
Scenario: Overseas University Visa Bond Emergency Transfer (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: URGENCY_OR_THREAT, FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION, ISOLATION_OR_PSYCHOLOGICAL_PRESSURE

[CALLER]: This is Officer David calling from Consulate Visa Cell. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding student visa documentation discrepancy for dependent child.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any student visa documentation discrepancy for dependent child. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be immediate deportation and visa cancellation.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with embassy security deposit transfer. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
