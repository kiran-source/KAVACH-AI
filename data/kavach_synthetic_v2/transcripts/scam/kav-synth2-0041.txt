=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0041 (en) ===
Scenario: KBC Lucky Draw Processing Fee Advance Fraud (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION, OTP_PIN_CVV_THEFT, URGENCY_OR_THREAT

[CALLER]: This is Officer Rana Pratap calling from KBC Lottery Office. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding 25 Lakhs cash prize in national SIM draw.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any 25 Lakhs cash prize in national SIM draw. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be cancellation of winning check.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with 12,500 rupees GST processing charge. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
