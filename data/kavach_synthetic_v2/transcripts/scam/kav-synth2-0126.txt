=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0126 (en) ===
Scenario: Hospital Intensive Care Emergency Cash Extortion (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: URGENCY_OR_THREAT, FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION, ISOLATION_OR_PSYCHOLOGICAL_PRESSURE

[CALLER]: This is Dr. Alok calling from City Care Hospital. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding relative admitted unconscious after highway collision.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any relative admitted unconscious after highway collision. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be withholding of emergency blood transfusion surgery.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with 25,000 rupees medical deposit via phone. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
