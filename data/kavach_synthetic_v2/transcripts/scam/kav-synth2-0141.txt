=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0141 (en) ===
Scenario: National Highway Toll Penalty & Vehicle Confiscation (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: AUTHORITY_IMPERSONATION, URGENCY_OR_THREAT, FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION

[CALLER]: This is Inspector Tiwari calling from NHAI Legal Enforcement. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding toll gate sensor evasion and unpaid toll fine of 8,500.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any toll gate sensor evasion and unpaid toll fine of 8,500. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be impounding of vehicle at next police barrier.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with urgent penalty payment through unofficial link. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
