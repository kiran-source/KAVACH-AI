=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0086 (en) ===
Scenario: Work From Home YouTube Liking Deposit Scheme (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION, URGENCY_OR_THREAT

[CALLER]: This is Recruiter Sneha calling from Global Media Marketing. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding daily guaranteed earnings of 3,500 rupees for video likes.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any daily guaranteed earnings of 3,500 rupees for video likes. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be loss of assigned investment portfolio slot.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with 5,000 rupees VIP task security deposit. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
