=== CONVERSATION: KAV-SYNTH2-0116 (en) ===
Scenario: Fake Job Appointment Letter Registration Fee Advance (EN)
Label: SCAM
Tactics: FINANCIAL_REDIRECTION, URGENCY_OR_THREAT

[CALLER]: This is HR Lead Nambiar calling from Tata Tech Recruitment. We are conducting an urgent investigation regarding selection as Senior Systems Engineer with 12 LPA package.
[RECIPIENT]: Hello? I have no knowledge of any selection as Senior Systems Engineer with 12 LPA package. What is this regarding?
[CALLER]: A formal case file has been registered. If you do not resolve this immediately, there will be cancellation of appointment offer letter.
[RECIPIENT]: Please, this is a serious misunderstanding! I am a law-abiding citizen. How can this be cleared?
[CALLER]: You must immediately comply with 8,500 rupees document verification charge. Failure to do so will result in non-bailable enforcement.
[RECIPIENT]: I will not comply blindly without official documentation. Let me consult my advocate and local police first.
